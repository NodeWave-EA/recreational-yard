## Recreation Yard Runners: Move. Play. Connect. Thrive.

Recreation Yard Runners is built on the belief that **every step counts**. Through morning group runs in Kericho, virtual distance challenges, and marathon preparation, our community supports runners, joggers, and walkers of all backgrounds and paces.
