## Recreation & Group Experiences

At **Recreation Yard Kericho**, located at KOOMA along the Kericho–Kisumu Highway, we provide an outdoor environment where groups, families, corporate teams, and schools can interact, participate in recreational activities, and enjoy memorable shared experiences away from the formal workplace or daily routine.

Because every group has unique objectives, schedules, and sizes, our pricing is **requirements-led and quoted individually**. We discuss your preferred activities, group numbers, seating requirements, and duration before issuing a transparent quotation.

### Experiences We Host

#### 1. Corporate Team Building

Give your team a refreshing break in an open highland setting. Programmes can incorporate:

- **Cornhole challenges and tournaments**
- **Badminton and lawn tennis on grass**
- **Supervised archery target sessions**
- **Outdoor chess and strategic challenges**
- **Recreational running, jogging, and walking group activities**

#### 2. Birthday Parties & Graduation Celebrations

Host small-to-medium-sized social celebrations:

- Children's and adults' birthday parties
- Academic graduation celebrations with family and friends
- Family reunions and social club get-togethers
- Bring your own food, graduation cakes, and permitted refreshments

#### 3. Bring-Your-Own-Refreshments Picnics

Plan a casual or structured outdoor picnic with friends or family, pairing fresh highland air with active lawn games.

#### 4. Meetings & Social Gatherings

A scenic alternative to indoor conference rooms for community groups, running clubs, and small business strategy sessions.

### How to Book & Request a Quote

1. **Submit Your Inquiry:** Share your preferred date, estimated participant count, and intended activities through our [contact form](/contact?type=group-booking) or call/WhatsApp `0792035035`.
2. **Consultation & Programme Design:** We advise on venue suitability, equipment availability, and agree on timing.
3. **Quotation & Confirmation:** We provide a transparent quote covering your agreed package. Your booking is confirmed upon agreement and scheduling.
