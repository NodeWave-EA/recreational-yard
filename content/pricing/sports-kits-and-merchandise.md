## Sports Kits & Merchandise Supply

Through **Recreation Yard Limited**, we supply high-quality sports uniforms, running apparel, and selected sporting equipment to individuals, schools, corporate organizations, running clubs, and event organizers across Kenya.

Whether you need a complete marathon kit for your next race, tailored school games uniforms for an entire student body, or branded corporate sportswear for an upcoming tournament, we coordinate specifications, sizes, quantities, branding, and delivery arrangements to deliver dependable quality within your budget.

### Product & Supply Categories

#### 1. Running Kits & Complete Marathon Kits

Tailored for individual marathoners, running clubs, and corporate wellness initiatives:

- **Running T-Shirts & Performance Tees:** Lightweight, breathable, moisture-wicking fabrics.
- **Running Shorts & Outerwear:** Ergonomic shorts and wind-resistant running jackets.
- **Running Shoes & Drawstring Bags:** Reliable footwear and convenient gear storage.
- **Complete Marathon Packages:** Bundled kits tailored to participant needs and event branding.

#### 2. School Games Kits

Comprehensive sports uniform solutions for primary and secondary schools:

- School games kit uniforms and physical education gear.
- Team jerseys for football, netball, volleyball, and athletics.
- Custom printing with school crests, house colours, and student numbers.

#### 3. Corporate Sportswear & Branded Apparel

Professional athletic wear for company sports teams and health days:

- Corporate athletic polos, moisture-wicking tees, and tracksuits.
- Custom company logo embroidery and screen printing.
- Coordinated staff sports day kits.

#### 4. Selected Sporting Equipment

- Match and training footballs and sports balls.
- Badminton and lawn tennis rackets, shuttlecocks, and balls.
- Cornhole board sets, bean bags, and lawn game accessories.
