## About the Event

**#WeRunSundays** is a weekly virtual fitness community initiative organized by **Recreation Yard Runners** in Kericho. Whether you are a beginner taking your first steps or an experienced runner logging weekly mileage, this event is built to keep our community active, healthy, and connected every Sunday.

### How to Participate

1. **Sign Up for Free:** Click the registration button or scan the QR code to join the official Recreation Yard Runners WhatsApp group.
2. **Choose Your Route:** Run, jog, or walk anywhere around Kericho town, along local trails, or in your home neighborhood.
3. **Track Your Movement:** Use Strava, Nike Run Club, Garmin, or any fitness tracker of your choice.
4. **Share Your Journey:** Post your stats and photos in the group to get featured on the weekly community leaderboard!

---

### Facility & Community Features

* **Fitness & Running:** Open to all ages, fitness levels, and distance preferences.
* **Community Wellness:** Regular social interaction, motivation, and active living tips.
* **Recreation Yard Amenities:** Connect with us for badminton, tennis (Da Sclup Lawn), archery, lawn games, and venue hosting.
