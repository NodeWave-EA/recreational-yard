## Group & Corporate Experiences at Recreation Yard Kericho

At **Recreation Yard Kericho**, located at KOOMA along the Kericho–Kisumu Highway, we provide a green, open-air environment where teams, families, and community organizations can escape the confines of indoor rooms and connect through active recreation and shared experiences.

Whether you are planning a corporate retreat to re-energize staff, an unforgettable outdoor birthday party, an academic graduation celebration, or an informal association meeting, we tailor our venue arrangements to meet your goals.

### What We Offer for Groups

#### 1. Corporate Team Building & Staff Wellness

Step out of the office and foster genuine collaboration through activities that promote team spirit, communication, and friendly competition:

- **Cornhole Tournaments:** Highly engaging, accessible team toss competitions.
- **Badminton & Lawn Tennis on Grass:** Singles and doubles rallies that get heart rates up.
- **Supervised Archery:** Structured target shooting sessions with complete safety oversight.
- **Outdoor Chess:** Strategic matches that spark thoughtful discussions.
- **Group Running, Jogging & Walking:** Coordinated wellness loops around our scenic grounds.

#### 2. Birthday Parties & Graduation Celebrations

Celebrate life milestones surrounded by lush greenery. Organize your own food, graduation cakes, and permitted refreshments while guests enjoy lawn games and relaxation.

#### 3. Informal Meetings & Community Gatherings

An uplifting open-air alternative for sports clubs, neighbourhood associations, and small business strategy sessions.

### Advance Planning & Booking Process

To guarantee safety, comfort, and attentive coordination, **prior booking is mandatory for all group experiences**.

1. **Reach Out:** Contact us with your preferred date, anticipated attendance, and event type.
2. **Customized Programme:** We discuss available activities, seating arrangements, and timing.
3. **Quotation & Confirmation:** We provide a transparent quote and reserve your private lawn space upon confirmation.
