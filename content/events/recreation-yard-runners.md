## RUN. JOG. WALK. ENJOY THE JOURNEY.

**Recreation Yard Runners** is the community running and active living brand of **Recreation Yard Limited**. We promote recreational running and walking through a warm, welcoming community that encourages regular movement, consistency, personal progress, and genuine social connection.

We believe that **every step counts**. You do not need to be an elite athlete or competitive sprinter to belong here. Whether you walk for daily wellness, jog at an easy pace to unwind after work, or log high mileage preparing for your next marathon, our community is built for you.

### Who We Welcome

- **Beginners:** Taking their first steps into walking, brisk walking, or run-walk intervals.
- **Recreational Joggers:** Looking for an enjoyable, moderate-paced fitness routine with supportive peers.
- **Experienced Runners:** Pursuing personal bests, endurance milestones, and marathon goals.
- **Walking Enthusiasts:** Seeking low-impact, accessible exercise and outdoor conversation.
- **Virtual Participants:** Completing activities in their own home towns while taking part in our club challenges and receiving club kits.

### Key Club Activities

1. **Physical Meet-ups in Kericho:** Scheduled morning runs and walks starting around KOOMA along the scenic Kericho–Kisumu Highway, taking advantage of the clean highland air and tea estate greenery.
2. **Virtual Community Participation:** Members outside Kericho can join our agreed monthly distance challenges, share their progress, and celebrate milestones together.
3. **Marathon Preparation:** Supportive group pacing, tips on pacing strategy, hydration, and collective motivation for major road race events.
4. **Coordinated Running Apparel:** Complete marathon kits (performance tees, running shorts, running jackets, shoes, drawstring bags, and custom team uniforms) supplied through Recreation Yard Limited.
