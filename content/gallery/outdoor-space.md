## Welcome to Recreation Yard Kericho

Recreation Yard Kericho is your space for recreation, active living, sports, and shared experiences. Located at KOOMA along the Kericho–Kisumu Highway, our grounds offer space for lawn games, bring-your-own-refreshments picnics, outdoor celebrations, informal meetings, and corporate team building. Prior booking is mandatory.
