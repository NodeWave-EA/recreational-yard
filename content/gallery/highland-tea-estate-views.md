## Kericho Highland Landscape & Setting

Recreation Yard Kericho is set against the renowned green countryside of Kericho County. Located at KOOMA along the Kericho–Kisumu Highway, our open venue offers visitors clean air, sweeping greenery, and an inspiring atmosphere for recreation, sports, and active living.
