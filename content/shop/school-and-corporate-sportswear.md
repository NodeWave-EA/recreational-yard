## School Games Kits & Corporate Sportswear

**Recreation Yard Limited** is a dependable supplier of sports uniforms, team kits, and corporate sportswear for primary and secondary schools, corporate organizations, sports clubs, community leagues, and tournament organizers.

We believe that high-quality, comfortable team kits inspire pride, unity, and confidence on the field. We work closely with school administrators, sports teachers, and corporate team managers to deliver tailored sports apparel that stands up to regular training and competition.

### Product Offerings

#### 1. School Games Kit Uniforms

- Complete school sports kits for inter-house competitions, physical education, and inter-school tournaments.
- Match jerseys and shorts for football, netball, volleyball, basketball, and athletics.
- Custom house colours, school crest embroidery, student numbering, and durable stitching.

#### 2. Corporate Sportswear & Team Uniforms

- Branded athletic polo shirts, technical dry-fit T-shirts, and tracksuits for company teams.
- Coordinated sports day kits for annual corporate tournaments and inter-branch games.
- High-quality company logo printing and embroidery that aligns with brand guidelines.

#### 3. Sports Clubs & Community Teams

- Uniforms and training kits for community football clubs, youth academies, and local sports associations.
- Warm-up tops, bibs, and coach gear.

### How We Deliver

We work directly with customers to determine:

- **Specifications:** Fabric weight, breathability, and cuts.
- **Visuals & Branding:** Team crests, sponsor logos, numbers, and colours.
- **Quantities & Sizing:** Complete size breakdowns from youth sizes to adult XL.
- **Budget & Schedules:** Transparent quotations and confirmed delivery timelines.
