## Outdoor Chess, Darts & Social Gatherings

At **Recreation Yard Kericho**, located at KOOMA along the Kericho–Kisumu Highway, recreation spans both physical energy and relaxed mental engagement. Our dedicated board and social games area features outdoor chess and darts (subject to the venue's safety arrangements), surrounded by open greenery.

In addition to leisurely gaming, our green outdoor setting offers a refreshing alternative to traditional indoor conference rooms for small-to-medium-sized informal meetings and social gatherings.

### Board & Social Games

- **Outdoor Chess:** Challenge friends, family members, or colleagues to a strategic match under the open highland sky. Chess is a wonderful way to relax, focus, and connect across generations.
- **Darts:** Test precision and aim in our designated darts zone. To protect the well-being of all guests, darts activities are conducted strictly subject to the venue's safety arrangements and adult oversight.

### Meetings & Group Get-Togethers

Looking for an inspiring open-air environment to hold your next discussion? We accommodate:

- **Community Meetings & Local Associations:** Open, accessible outdoor space for community members to meet and deliberate.
- **Club & Sports Group Gatherings:** Planning sessions for running clubs, cycling teams, and local athletic groups.
- **Small Business & Team Retreats:** Step away from the office desk to hold informal brainstorming and planning discussions.
- **Networking & Social Mixers:** Casual outdoor get-togethers where participants can talk, connect, and enjoy lawn games.

### Advance Planning & Booking

All meetings and game area visits require **prior booking and confirmation**. When contacting our team, please share your preferred date, expected attendance, seating needs, and any special requirements so that we can advise on venue suitability, layout, and availability.
