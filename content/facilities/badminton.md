## Badminton on the Lawn

Badminton is one of our primary lawn games at **Recreation Yard Kericho**, located at KOOMA along the Kericho–Kisumu Highway. Played outdoors on a dedicated grass playing area, badminton offers an enjoyable, energetic, and accessible game for participants of all ages and fitness levels.

Whether you are looking for friendly rallies with friends, a casual family activity, or competitive challenges as part of a corporate team-building programme, our outdoor recreation space is designed to help you move, play, connect, and thrive.

### Who It Is For

- **Individuals & Friends:** Recreational matches and active outdoor exercise.
- **Families:** Engaging, multi-generational play in a relaxed green environment.
- **Corporate Groups:** Friendly competitions, round-robin challenges, and icebreaker activities during team building.
- **Schools & Sports Clubs:** Structured outdoor recreation sessions and physical activity meet-ups.

### Booking & Preparation

Prior booking is mandatory for all visits and activities at Recreation Yard Kericho. Please contact us in advance with your preferred date, expected number of players, and equipment requirements so that we can confirm availability and ensure your slot is reserved.
