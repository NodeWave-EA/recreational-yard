## Cornhole & Active Lawn Games

Cornhole has become one of the most popular and social lawn games at **Recreation Yard Kericho**, located at KOOMA along the Kericho–Kisumu Highway. Set up across our manicured green lawns, cornhole offers an accessible, high-spirited tossing game that combines skill, strategy, and plenty of laughs.

Because cornhole has simple rules and requires no heavy athletic background, it is an exceptional equalizer where children, parents, corporate executives, and first-time players can compete on equal footing.

### How It Fits into Your Visit

- **Corporate Team Building:** Cornhole bracket tournaments and toss shootouts serve as outstanding icebreakers that foster teamwork, communication, and friendly rivalry.
- **Birthday Parties & Graduations:** Keep guests actively engaged between refreshments with ongoing casual matches.
- **Family Outings:** A favorite lawn game where siblings, parents, and grandparents can play together in the open air.
- **Lawn Game Combos:** Combine cornhole with lawn tennis, badminton, and supervised archery for a multi-sport recreational day.

### Planning Your Games Day

All lawn game sets are coordinated for reserved visits. **Prior booking is strictly mandatory**. When contacting us, let us know your group size and intended activities so we can reserve your playing lanes and equipment.
