## Running, Jogging & Walking with Recreation Yard Runners

Under our dedicated running and active living brand, **Recreation Yard Runners**, we promote recreational running, jogging, and walking through a welcoming community that encourages movement, consistency, personal progress, and social connection.

Kericho's clean air, gentle altitude, and vibrant greenery provide the perfect backdrop for morning runs, endurance training, or refreshing weekend nature walks.

### RUN. JOG. WALK. ENJOY THE JOURNEY.

Our community is built around the philosophy that **every step counts**. We welcome:

- **Beginners:** Taking their first steps into walking, brisk walking, or light jogging.
- **Recreational Joggers:** Looking for an enjoyable, moderate-paced fitness routine with supportive peers.
- **Experienced Runners:** Training for distance milestones, local road races, and full marathons.
- **Walking Enthusiasts:** Seeking low-impact, health-boosting leisure and outdoor fellowship.
- **Virtual Participants:** Completing activity and distance challenges in their own home locations while staying connected.

### Running Activities & Club Highlights

- **Physical Meet-ups in Kericho:** Scheduled group runs and walks starting or passing through KOOMA and scenic highland routes.
- **Virtual Challenges:** Agreed monthly distance goals that allow members anywhere in Kenya and abroad to take part.
- **Marathon Preparation:** Supportive group pacing and training guidance for participants entering competitive or charity runs.
- **Complete Running Kits:** Coordinated running shirts, shorts, jackets, running shoes, and drawstring bags through Recreation Yard Limited.

### Get Involved

Whether you are joining our next scheduled Kericho group meet-up or looking to order branded running kits for your company or team, contact us today to join the journey.
