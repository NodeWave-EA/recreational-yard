1. WHO WE ARE 
We are a recreation, sports, wellness, events and sports merchandise business committed to making active living 
and outdoor experiences more accessible and enjoyable. 
Through our two brands and three complementary business areas, we bring together: 
• Outdoor recreation: A green space for lawn games, board games, picnics and social experiences. 
• Running and community: Physical and virtual running, jogging and walking through our recreational 
running club. 
• Sports merchandise: Running apparel, complete marathon kits, school games uniforms, corporate 
sportswear and selected sporting equipment. 
• Events and group experiences: Outdoor birthday parties, graduations, meetings and team-building 
activities. 
We serve individuals, families, schools, corporate organizations, sports clubs, event organizers and community 
groups. 
Our approach is simple: we create opportunities for people to move, play, connect, relax and thrive through 
recreation, sport and shared experiences.
